# Radio Atlas

Radio Atlas helps you discover radio stations, find their local frequencies and choose how to listen online.

Search by station, location, frequency or content, or browse by country, city and broadcast type. Each station includes a description, listening links and, where available, its programming, history and contact details. Sources and verification dates help you assess the information.

Play compatible streams directly in Radio Atlas, with one station playing at a time and native audio controls. A listening-page link remains available when a stream cannot play in your browser. Save favorites on your device and combine them with location, content and broadcast filters, or copy a frequency for your radio.

A compact player keeps playback controls within reach while you browse, when screen space permits. Expand it to return to the full player, or use Back to filters after the results to change your search.

The compact listening guide explains audio formats, data usage and radio frequencies. The catalog covers stations from several countries and is available in English and Romanian.

Radio Atlas is an independent catalog. Station availability can change; unconfirmed information and playback limitations are noted in the listings.
