# Radio Atlas

Radio Atlas helps you discover radio stations, find their local frequencies and choose how to listen online.

Search by station, location, frequency or content, or browse by country, city and broadcast type. Each station includes a description, listening links and, where available, its programming, history and contact details. Sources and verification dates help you assess the information.

Play compatible streams directly in Radio Atlas, with one station playing at a time and native audio controls. A listening-page link remains available when a stream cannot play in your browser. Use Previous station and Next station to cycle through browser-playable stations in your filtered results, including favorites. Filtering keeps the current station selected.

Save favorites on your device, access them from compact presets above the player, and combine them with location, content and broadcast filters, or copy a frequency for your radio.

Radio Atlas remembers your last selected station, volume and mute setting on this device. Reloading restores the selection without loading a stream; press Play to resume.

A compact player keeps playback controls within reach while you browse, when screen space permits. Expand it to return to the full player, or use Back to filters after the results to change your search.

Choose a location and open Browse the FM dial to explore its catalog frequencies from 87.5 to 108 MHz. Select a frequency entry to open its station card and online listening options. This is a catalog view; it does not scan or receive radio signals.

The compact listening guide explains audio formats, data usage and radio frequencies. The catalog covers stations from several countries and is available in English and Romanian.

Radio Atlas is an independent catalog. Station availability can change; unconfirmed information and playback limitations are noted in the listings.
